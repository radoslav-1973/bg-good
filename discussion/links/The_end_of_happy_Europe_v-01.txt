<h5>The End of Happy Europe</h5>
<p>(The name is borrowed from Deutsche Welle's<a target="_blank" href="https://www.dw.com/bg/kraat-na-sastlivata-evropa/a-72041681"> anti-utopia </a>
of the same name)</p><br>
<p>The Bulgarian office of "Deutsche Welle" actively contributes to "The End of Happy Europe".</p>
<p>The journalists' links with the SIC are the least of the problems, if behind the facade of a state-owned and authoritative editorial office 
there was not the corrupt aim of compromising the European high-tech plans for project and building of small modular reactors and the future 
of nuclear power as a whole. But who is in favor of that an objective, given that if all nuclear power plants in France, were shut down like 
Germany, the price of methane would soar manifold. Certainly, China or Turkey are not among the countries that have an interest in such 
price rises, but this is not profitable for Europe either. For whom then does the Bulgarian office of Deutsche Welle work, together with the SIC and 
the Bulgarian Socialist Party (PP-DB), is a question of reduced difficulty. The answer is elementary, but the possible consequences of such a 
lobbying policy would be exponential, complex and complicated for the whole of Europe, for all aspects of economic and social life.</p>
<p>But after all, are the lobbyists behind "Nord Stream 1" and "Nord Stream 2" so powerful as to eliminate all possibilities for technological 
development in Europe and practically destroy it by pushing it towards misery, separatism and chaos?!</p>
<p>Such a script is not in interest of the President of the Russian Federation either, because Europe is not Russia's backyard like Chechnya, 
and any total destabilization will have the potential, sooner or later, to affect the Russian Federation itself.</p>
